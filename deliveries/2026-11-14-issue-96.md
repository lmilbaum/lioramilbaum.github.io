## Why

_From the PR description (author-written)._

Day 7 of the Qodo Software Factory hackathon ([discussion #9](https://github.com/orgs/qodo-hackathon/discussions/9)) asks for a PR's Qodo findings to be worked through with the Review Resolver skill. PR #97 (closes #72) went through seven Qodo reviews and twelve findings: ten fixed, two dismissed as false positives. Its final head lists no open finding. The results are posted at [the discussion comment](https://github.com/qodo-hackathon/software-factory-hackathon/discussions/9#discussioncomment-19618604). Issue #96's last criterion is that the website lists the submission.

## How

_From the PR description (author-written)._

This follows the same pattern as Day 6 (#83): the page entry, an exact-URL assertion in `check-site.sh`, and a check of `check-links.sh`'s time budget. The link uses the repo form (`github.com/qodo-hackathon/software-factory-hackathon/discussions/9#…`), like Days 1–6, because `check-links.sh`'s comment lookup reads owner/repo from the URL. Day 7 brings the jobs to 20 (13 links and 7 comment lookups). The cap of 10 still fits them:
- batch 1 is 10 plain links, at most 66 s;
- batch 2 is 3 links plus 7 lookups, at most 198 s;
- the worst case is 264 s, inside the step's 5-minute timeout.

The cap is unchanged; only `check-links.sh`'s comment is updated, including a stale "batches of 8" from before Day 6.

- `scripts/check-links.sh`: the budget comment only (20 jobs, two full batches).
